Connecting people and opportunity

Our Mission

Stronger communities and industries

We bring people, educators, and employers together to strengthen communities and local industries across Eastern North Carolina.

Our Vision

A clear path to opportunity

We envision an Eastern North Carolina where people have a clear path to a career and businesses have the talent they need to grow.

Our Approach

Industry, education, and talent

We build partnerships, use regional data, and connect learning to the needs of local employers.
