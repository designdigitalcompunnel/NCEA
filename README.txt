NC EAST ALLIANCE — TWO-PAGE WEBSITE CONCEPT

Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a web browser.
Use the Our Work navigation link to open our-work.html.

The project is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Its photos, posters, and
videos are included. The template's original Google Fonts load when
available, and system fonts provide an offline fallback. Links to official
programs, news, directions, or partner sites require an internet connection.

The News & Updates form opens an email draft using the visitor's mail app.
It does not collect addresses or subscribe visitors through a mailing-list service.

Calendar dates and program descriptions are based on NC East Alliance's
public information and the project brief. Photos and videos are illustrative.
